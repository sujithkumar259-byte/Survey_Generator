# Cross-cutting PMR best practices — ALWAYS injected into the section-suggester
# and question-drafter, in addition to any study-type-specific guidance.
# '#' lines are comments and are stripped before the model sees this.

QUESTIONNAIRE STRUCTURE — KEEP IT TIGHT
- Aim for 5-6 thematic sections PLUS the screener — not a dozen. Consolidate
  related topics into one substantial section (e.g. drivers + barriers together;
  attitudes + perceptions together; current behaviour + share together) rather
  than creating a separate section per hypothesis or sub-topic. A professional
  questionnaire has a handful of meaty sections, not many thin ones.

PATIENT VOLUMES & COUNTS — OPEN NUMERIC, NOT BUCKETS
- Capture volumes and counts as open NUMERIC entry, e.g. "In a typical month,
  approximately how many patients with <condition> do you personally manage?
  ___". Do NOT pre-bucket them into single-select ranges (0-10, 11-20, ...) —
  bucketing discards analyzable continuous data and reads as amateur.
- When a volume is a screening threshold, still capture it as NUMERIC and apply
  the cut-off in the question's termination logic — never as buckets.

TREATMENT / PATIENT SHARE — A PERCENT_ALLOCATION GRID
- For "what share / proportion of your patients are treated with each therapy",
  use ONE PERCENT_ALLOCATION grid, not a series of separate questions:
  - Rows = the treatment options. RANDOMIZE the substantive treatments; ANCHOR
    "Untreated / watchful waiting" and "Other (please specify: ___)" at the
    bottom (do not randomize the anchors).
  - grid_columns = the relevant patient segments, each shown conditionally
    (SHOW IF that segment applies to the respondent).
  - Each column must sum to 100% (or "at least 100%" when combination therapy is
    allowed). Include a running total / AUTOSUM and an explicit "each column must
    sum to 100%" instruction.
  - Put RANDOMIZE / ANCHOR / AUTOSUM / running-total in programming_instructions;
    put the SHOW-IF conditions in option_level_logic. Phrase the stem like:
    "In the past 12 months, approximately what proportion of your <patients> were
    treated with the following?"
