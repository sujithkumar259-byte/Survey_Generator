### SYSTEM
{shared_system_context}

You are designing the SKELETON of a market research questionnaire: the ordered
list of sections. A good questionnaire is more than a container for hypotheses -
it must also stand on its own as a coherent respondent experience.

### USER
Propose the complete, ordered list of survey sections for this study.

{best_practices}

Two kinds of sections must appear:

1. STANDARD STRUCTURAL SECTIONS - always include these regardless of whether any
   hypothesis names them, because every professional questionnaire needs them:
   - Screener / eligibility (section_id "SCR") - always first.
   - Respondent profile / professional background (specialty, years in practice,
     setting, patient volume) - needed to describe and segment the sample.
   - Sources of information / context-setting, where relevant to the study.
   - Awareness / usage or current behaviour, where relevant.
   - Closing / classification / firmographics - always last.

2. HYPOTHESIS-DRIVEN SECTIONS - the thematic sections needed so that every
   approved hypothesis has a natural home (drivers, barriers, attitudes,
   preferences, future intentions, etc.).

STUDY SETUP:
{study_block}

APPROVED HYPOTHESES:
{hyp_block}

Guidance:
- Keep the structure tight: aim for 5-6 thematic sections PLUS the screener — not
  a dozen. Consolidate related themes into one substantial section rather than one
  section per hypothesis or sub-topic (see the best-practices note above).
- Order sections the way a respondent should experience them: screener first,
  then warm-up / behaviour, then attitudinal / evaluative, then forward-looking,
  then classification last.
- Place unaided / spontaneous topics BEFORE aided / prompted ones.
- Keep section_id short and stable (SCR, A, B, C, ...). Screener is always "SCR".
- Section titles should be human-readable (e.g. "Section A: Adoption drivers").
- It is expected and correct to propose sections that no single hypothesis names
  (screener, profile, classification). Mark those with "is_must_have": true.

Return ONLY a JSON array of section objects, in display order:
[{{"section_id": "SCR", "section_title": "Section 0: Screener",
   "rationale": "why this section is needed", "is_must_have": true}}]
