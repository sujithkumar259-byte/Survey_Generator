CHECKLIST
- Attitudinal items are single-idea statements (never double-barrelled), so they
  can load onto one factor.
- Items are discriminating, not consensus statements everyone agrees with.
- Wording is neutral / non-leading and not emotionally polarising.
- The battery has balanced coverage of the hypothesised segmentation axes
  (e.g. drivers, barriers, risk attitude, innovation orientation).
- Rating scales are consistent across the battery (same 7-point scale, same
  anchors) so items are comparable.
- There are enough discriminating items to support a robust typing tool.
