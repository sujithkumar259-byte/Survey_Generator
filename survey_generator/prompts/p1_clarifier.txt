### USER
Read the brief and uploaded source material, then ask the clarifying questions
that would most improve the hypotheses you will generate next.

ANALYST BRIEF:
{brief_block}

FRAMEWORK BLOCKS THE ANALYST SELECTED:
{frameworks}

{files_block}
